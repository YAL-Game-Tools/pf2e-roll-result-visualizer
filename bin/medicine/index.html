About this tool

Can you tell on a glance whether you should roll DC 15 or DC 20 Battle Medicine? What if the target has Robust Health? What if you also have the Medic archetype? And what about Assurance? This little calculator aims to answer many such questions by showing you how likely any given DC is to succeed and what average healing would be.

See AoN for full rules.
